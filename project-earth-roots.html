How can a former shipyard find new life without losing its history?

The Jiangzhou proposal brings industrial heritage, ecological restoration and future community uses into one landscape strategy. Historical research and GIS analysis support a master plan that considers both the site's working past and its changing role.

My contribution

GIS analysis, historical research, concept, master plan, rendering, ecological and development strategies, and interface design.

Project team

Shuyu Lei, Chen Yunxia, Yang Tingting, Zhang Jingwen and Mai Qingkun. Shenzhen University.
