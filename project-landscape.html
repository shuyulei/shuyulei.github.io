What can a campus landscape offer beyond a route between buildings?

Interviews and site analysis informed a renewal proposal for the architecture school landscape. Planting, lighting and spatial design explore how the grounds can support meeting, resting and everyday use as well as movement.

My contribution

Interviews, site analysis, design concept, master plan, rendering and lighting design.
